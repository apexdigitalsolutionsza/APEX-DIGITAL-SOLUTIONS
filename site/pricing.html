Questions

Straight answers

Why aren't prices listed on the site?

Because an honest number needs five minutes of context. A three-page site for a sole trader and a twenty-page site with booking and automation are very different jobs. We quote on the actual scope so the figure you get is the figure you pay — no "from" pricing that triples once we start.

How long does a website take?

A Starter site is typically ready in about 1–2 weeks, Business in 2–4 weeks, and Growth projects 3–6 weeks depending on scope. The biggest variable is how quickly we get your content — photos, service details and logo.

What do you need from me to get started?

Your logo if you have one, a few photos of your work, a list of your services and areas you cover, and your contact details. If you don't have good photos, we'll advise on what to shoot or source.

Do I own my website?

Yes. The website, domain and content are yours. If you ever move elsewhere, we hand everything over cleanly — no hostage situations.

Do I have to take a monthly plan?

No. Hosting and maintenance are optional. Most clients take it because they'd rather not deal with renewals, backups and updates, but you're free to host wherever you like.

Do you work with businesses outside Gauteng?

Yes. We work with businesses across South Africa. Everything can be handled by WhatsApp, email and video call, and the process works exactly the same wherever you are.
